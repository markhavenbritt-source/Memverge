Your AI should start with you.

Alex Morgan · September 22, 2026

A more personal starting point

Every project carries a history: the idea that sparked it, the choices you’ve already made, and the details only you know. Yet a new AI conversation can feel like starting from a blank page.

A more personal relationship with AI begins with that context. Not just the question you ask today, but the thinking that brought you here.

Context makes the difference

Imagine working on a presentation. The useful starting point isn’t simply “make me slides.” It’s the audience you care about, the story you want to tell, and the decisions your team has already made.

Gathering those pieces helps you express what matters. A short brief, a few notes, or an example of work you love can give a conversation direction before the first draft.

Continuity leaves room to create

Repeating the same background takes time and interrupts your thinking. A useful foundation of context lets you spend more of a conversation exploring possibilities and refining the work.

That foundation should evolve with you. Projects change. Preferences shift. The context that helps today may need a different shape tomorrow.

Your memory. Your ground rules.

Personal context deserves personal control. Where does it live? What gets used? Can you see it, change it, or decide that it no longer belongs? Those are essential questions for any AI experience.

Our vision for MemoryBox starts with the person at the center. Technology should help you feel more capable, with your memory, context, and intent powering work that is unmistakably yours.

Start with what matters to you

Choose one project you’re working on. Write down its purpose, the people it serves, and the decisions you want to carry forward. Add one example of what good looks like.

You don’t need to capture everything. Start with the details that would help a thoughtful collaborator understand the work. That is where a more personal AI experience begins.
